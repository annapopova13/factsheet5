Factsheet · Pension & mobility

RESAVER Pension Fund

Stay mobile, stay secure — a smarter way to save for retirement.

Researcher mobility drives excellence across the European Research Area — but moving to a new country too often means losing pension contributions, or missing out altogether during a short-term contract's vesting period. RESAVER (the Retirement Savings Vehicle for European Research Institutions) is the pan-European pension fund built to fix that: a single, non-profit, multi-employer fund tailor-made for research organisations, co-financed by the European Commission and regulated under the EU IORP II Directive.

5 things you need to know about RESAVER

The quick version — before you dive into the detail below.

01

Built for mobile researchers

Employees stay affiliated to the same supplementary pension fund when they move between countries and employers within the EEA — even though rights were accrued with different employers in different countries.

02

Open to all staff, not only researchers

Researchers with employment contracts, researchers arriving from outside the EEA, and other employees at the same organisation are all eligible to join.

03

Already operational in 9 countries

Belgium, the Netherlands, Italy, Austria, Hungary, Cyprus, France, Spain and Czechia — with the ambition to expand across every European country.

04

Transparent, with no hidden fees

Setup and regulatory approval cost nothing. Ongoing membership, member and asset-management fees are all published up front.

05

Two ways to invest

"Life Style" automatically de-risks contributions as retirement nears; "Free Style" lets participants build their own portfolio from cash, bonds and equity funds.

Payment forms at retirement

What participants can choose from depends on the social and labour law of the country where they retire. Early withdrawal is possible under specific circumstances in some countries.

Lump sum payment

A one-off cash payment equal to the full value of the participant's account at the time of retirement.

Life-long pension payment

The account balance is converted into a monthly pension for life. A guaranteed annuity pays a fixed amount each year; a variable annuity pays an amount that moves with the performance of the underlying assets.
